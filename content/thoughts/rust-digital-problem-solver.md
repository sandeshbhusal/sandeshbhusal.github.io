Recently, I've seen an uptick in the number of recruiters reaching out to me on LinkedIn about Rust job postings. Some of them are really cool - like the one where they are using Rust as a HLS tool to synthesize circuits for LLM acceleration. I haven't touched Rust professionally in 3 years now, although I go back to it from time to time (and it's always a joy to program in!)

Recently I saw a post by a person on Reddit saying they learned Rust, but they couldn't find any jobs - got me wondering if you need Rust experience to get Rust jobs (circular dependency?) -- anyways, there was a comment I liked 

> Don't tie yourself to a language, we are all digital problem solvers

Huh. I guess I am a digital problem solver then.
